# Getting started with 167 Hours

Thanks for testing 167 Hours. Setup takes about 15 minutes, and you only do it once. Claude does most of the work for you.

**Before you start, you'll need:**

- To be 18 or older and currently seeing a therapist.
- A paid Claude account (Pro or higher).
- A computer for setup. After that, you can use it anywhere, including your phone.

**Not a crisis service.** If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 or call 911.

---

## Part 1: Make a free Supabase account

Supabase is where your entries are stored. It's your own private account, and nobody else can see what's in it.

1. Go to **supabase.com** and click **Start your project**.
2. Sign up with Google, GitHub, or your email. It's free, and you won't need a credit card.
3. If it asks you to create an organization, name it anything (your first name is fine) and choose the **Free** plan.
4. That's it. You don't need to create anything else; Claude will do that.

## Part 2: Connect Supabase to Claude

1. Open **claude.ai** and sign in.
2. Click your name in the bottom left, then **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
3. Find **Supabase** and click **Connect**.
4. A Supabase window opens. Click **Authorize** (or **Allow**).
5. You should see Supabase marked as connected.

## Part 3: Add the 167 Hours plugin

1. In Claude, open **Customize** in the left sidebar, then the **Plugins** tab.
2. Click **Add marketplace**, then **Add from a repository**.
3. Paste: `dkluger103w/167-hours`
4. Find **167 Hours** in the list and click **Add**.

(If you were sent a file called `167-hours.plugin` instead, use **Upload** on the same Plugins tab and choose that file.)

## Part 4: Let Claude set everything up

1. Start a new chat.
2. Type: **set up 167 Hours**
3. Answer Claude's questions. It will build your private database, ask a few things about you and your therapy schedule, and offer to make a safety plan.

## Part 5: Make a 167 Hours project (recommended)

This keeps your journal chats in one place.

1. In Claude, click **Projects**, then **New project**. Name it **167 Hours**.
2. In the project's instructions, paste: *Use the 167 Hours skills for everything in this project. Journal what I share.*
3. Do all your journaling in chats inside this project.

---

## Using it

- **Just talk.** Type or use voice, any time, about anything: a rough morning, an argument, an urge, a win, how you slept.
- **"Keep that private"** keeps something out of your therapist's summary.
- **"Debrief my session"** right after therapy captures what you covered and any homework.
- **"Build my report"** before your session makes your therapist's summary. You see it first, and you send it yourself.
- **"Show my safety plan"** any time you need it.
