Your privacy, in plain English

Best on a computer. Please do this one-time setup in a web browser on a laptop or desktop. It's much easier there. After that, you'll use the Claude app on your phone every day.

Looks like you're on a phone. You're welcome to read along, but we strongly recommend switching to a computer before you start the setup steps.

This test is built so that nobody at 167 Hours can see anything you write. Here's how it works.

1

Your Claude account

You talk to Claude using your own account: the one you already have, upgraded to Pro, or a new one if you've never used Claude. Your conversations live there, the same as any Claude chat you have today.

2

Your own private database

What matters from your week is saved in a free Supabase account that you create and only you can sign in to. We never get a login, a password, or a copy.

3

Your therapist, only when you say so

Before a session, you get a summary to read first. You decide what's in it, and you send it yourself. Nothing is ever sent for you.

What we can see: nothing.

Not your conversations, not your journal, not your reports. We have no access to your Claude account or your database. It couldn't be more private.

Same privacy as using Claude today.

Claude, made by Anthropic, handles your chats under the same privacy terms as any conversation you already have with it. 167 Hours doesn't add anyone new to that picture.

So please don't hold back. Talk the way you would if nobody else were reading, because nobody else is.

Before you start

The one rule

You need a therapist.

167 Hours supports the therapy you already have. It never replaces it.

To do the setup, you'll need:

  • To be 18 or older
  • To be seeing a therapist right now
  • A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). If you were given a 167 Hours card, use it for this.
  • A computer with a web browser. No app to install.
  • About 15 minutes

Make a free Supabase account

Supabase is where your journal is stored. It's your own private account, so nobody else can see what's in it, including us.

  • Click Open Supabase below and sign up with your email (or Google). You don't need a GitHub account.
  • Choose a password and save it. Let your browser or password app save it when it offers. You'll need it if you ever sign back in to Supabase.
  • If you sign up with email, Supabase shows "Check your email." Open your inbox and click the link within 10 minutes.

Keep the Supabase tab open. The next step happens right there.

Open Supabase ↗

Create your Supabase organization

Right after you confirm your email, Supabase shows "Create a new organization." Fill it in like this:

  • Name: leave what Supabase fills in, or change it to your first name. It doesn't matter.
  • Type: choose Personal.
  • Plan: choose Free - $0/month. You won't be asked for a credit card.
  • Click the green Create organization button.

Skip the project form

Next, Supabase opens a "Create a new project" form. Don't fill it in. Scroll to the bottom and click Cancel. Claude creates your project for you in a later step.

Download the 167 Hours plugin

Add the plugin to Claude

  • Click Open Plugins page below. It opens Claude in a new browser tab. Sign in if asked.
  • You'll land on the Customize page with Plugins selected. It usually opens on the Discover tab, full of plugins from other companies. Ignore those.
  • Click Yours, right next to Discover. This is where your plugins live. It may be empty, or show one or two from Anthropic. That's fine.
  • At the top right, click the white + Add button, then Upload plugin. (Skip the "Build for the Claude Directory" box; that's for publishing plugins.)
  • Click to choose a file and pick 167-hours.plugin from your Downloads. If you downloaded it more than once, it may be called something like 167-hours (1).plugin. That's fine.
  • You'll see a note that says "Only upload plugins you trust." Claude shows that for every plugin you upload yourself. Under Preview, check that it says 167-hours, then click Upload.
  • You'll see the 167 hours plugin page, with the switch at the top right turned on (blue). Back on Yours, 167 hours is now in your list.
  • On the right it says "Can send data to other services" and Supabase. That's your own private Supabase database from the earlier steps, and nothing else.
  • Don't click "Try in chat" yet. Turn on the two settings in the next step first.

If the button doesn't work, or you get lost: in Claude, open the left sidebar (the icon at the top left), click Customize, then the Plugins tab, then Yours.

Don't see a Plugins page? Plugins only show up on a paid plan. If you just upgraded, refresh the page, or sign out and back in.

Open Plugins page ↗

Turn on two Claude settings

These let Claude save your journal and make your therapist's summary without stopping to ask about every little thing.

  • Click Open Capabilities below (or in Claude: your name in the bottom left, then Settings, then Capabilities). These are usually on already. Just check them:
    • Under Code execution and file creation: Run code in the cloud should be on (blue). If it's off, click it.
    • Under Visuals: Artifacts should be on. It may look gray and dim. That's normal; it's locked on while "Run code in the cloud" is on.
    • Leave everything else as it is.
  • Start a brand-new chat: click + New at the top of the left sidebar. If you already have other chats in Claude, that's fine. Just don't reuse an old one. This new chat will become your 167 Hours journal.
  • Just under the chat box, on the right, next to the model name (like Opus 5.5), it says Auto or Manual. If it says Manual, click it and choose Auto. Without this, Claude asks permission for every step of setup.
  • Check the model, just left of Auto. It should say Opus 5.5. If it says something else, click it and choose Opus 5.5. It's the most capable model and works best for 167 Hours. Leave the effort setting next to it (like Medium) as it is.
  • Leave the Project menu on the left alone.

Let Claude set everything up

In that new chat (with Auto on), send this message:

set up 167 Hours

Claude asks for your therapist's name and confirms you're 18 or older. Then here's what you'll see:

  • A Supabase card with a Connect button appears in the chat. Click Connect.
  • If Supabase asks you to sign in first, that's normal. It happens when this browser isn't already signed in to Supabase. Sign in the same way you did when you made your account: with your email and the password you saved, or with Continue with Google if that's how you signed up. Then the Authorize page appears.
  • An "Authorize Claude" page opens (sometimes in a new browser tab or window). Under Organization, check it shows the one you made earlier (usually your email followed by 's Org). The permissions say Read + Write; that lets Claude build your journal inside your own Supabase account. Click the green Authorize Claude button. You'll come back to the chat.
  • Back in the chat, the card now says Connected with a Use button. Click Use. That sends "Use Supabase for this" for you, so there's nothing to type.
  • Claude builds your private journal on its own. This takes a minute or two.
  • It asks a few questions: what to call you, your time zone, when your sessions are, when you want your summary ready, your therapist's email, and which email you use. Then it offers to make a safety plan. "Later" is fine.

If a box pops up saying "Claude wants to use..." from Supabase, click Always allow. With Auto on, you shouldn't see many of these.

Pin your journal chat

The chat where you just did setup is now your journal. Keep using that one chat, every time, on your phone or computer. That way 167 Hours is always on.

  • Rename it 167 Hours (or "167 Hours" plus the name you gave it): tap or click the chat's title at the top (or the ... menu) and choose Rename.
  • Pin it: in the same menu, choose Pin, so it stays at the top of your chat list.
  • From now on, open the pinned 167 Hours chat whenever you want to talk. Long chats are fine; Claude condenses older parts on its own.

Started a different chat by accident? Just say "save this to 167 Hours" and it will.

You're all set.

Open your pinned 167 Hours chat in Claude any time, on your phone or computer, and just talk.

How it works

  • You don't change a thing. Talk to Claude the way you already would: about anything, any time, typing or voice.
  • You lead. 167 Hours won't quiz you, coach you, or give you homework. It listens.
  • It quietly saves what matters for your therapist in the background, so they see what your week was really like.
  • Get it on your phone. It's your choice, but most people use the free Claude app from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in with the same Claude account and your pinned 167 Hours chat is right there. Tap the microphone to talk.
  • Give it a name if you like. Setup asks if you'd like to name your 167 Hours companion. You can also say "call you [name]" any time to set or change it.
  • Forget something? Just say "help" in your 167 Hours chat and it'll explain everything again.

Finding your summary email

When you say "build my report" and approve it, 167 Hours saves an email to your therapist as a draft. A draft is an email that's written but not sent. It waits in your Drafts folder until you open it and press send.

Gmail

  • Phone: open the Gmail app, tap the menu (three lines, top left), then tap Drafts.
  • Computer: go to gmail.com and click Drafts in the list on the left.

Outlook (Outlook.com, Hotmail, Microsoft 365)

  • Phone: open the Outlook app, tap the menu or your picture (top left), then tap Drafts.
  • Computer: go to outlook.com and click Drafts in the folder list on the left.

Yahoo, Apple Mail, iCloud, AOL, or anything else

  • Claude can't save drafts there. Instead it gives you the email text in the chat. Copy it into a new email to your therapist.

Adding the PDF

  • Easiest, on your phone: in the Claude chat, tap the PDF, then tap the Share button and choose your email app (Gmail, Outlook or Mail). It opens a new email with the PDF already attached. Add your therapist's address and send. You can skip the draft entirely this way.
  • On a computer: click the PDF in the Claude chat to download it, open your draft, and drag the file from your Downloads folder into the email.

Look for the subject "Between-session summary". Open it, check that the "To" line is your therapist, drag in the PDF from the chat if you want it attached, and press send. Nothing is ever sent for you.

Updating 167 Hours

Your copy of 167 Hours doesn't update by itself. When there's a new version, we'll email you, and Claude will usually mention it in your journal chat too. Your journal entries are never affected; they stay in your own Supabase account.

  • Download the new plugin: 167-hours.plugin
  • In Claude, open Customize, then Plugins, and click Yours.
  • Click + Add, then Upload plugin, and choose the file you just downloaded.
  • Click Upload. A box pops up asking "Replace existing plugin?" Click Replace, the red button on the right. Don't press Enter or click Cancel; Cancel is highlighted by default, and it stops the update. Replace only swaps in the new version of 167 Hours. Your journal isn't touched.
  • The 167 hours plugin page now shows the new version number. That's it.

No rush. The older version keeps working; you can update whenever it's convenient.

If you get stuck
How do I use the 167 Hours card?

If you were sent card details for testing, use them to pay for Claude Pro. In Claude, open Settings, then Billing (or click Upgrade), choose the Pro plan billed monthly, and enter the card. It covers three months. After that, you can switch to your own card in the same place or cancel. The card only works for Claude, so it won't go through anywhere else.

I don't see Plugins or Customize in Claude

Plugins need a paid Claude plan. Check that you're signed in to the paid account, and try the Claude website or desktop app rather than the phone app for setup.

Claude says Supabase isn't connected

In Claude, go to Settings, then Connectors, find Supabase, and click Connect. Then start a brand-new chat (with Auto on) and send "set up 167 Hours" again. New connections show up in new chats.

Supabase says I've hit my free limit

Free Supabase accounts allow two active projects. If you already use Supabase for something else, tell the person who invited you to test and we'll sort it out.

Claude says I've reached a usage limit

Every Claude plan has limits that reset after a few hours. If you hit one, you can wait for it to reset, or, if it keeps happening, switch to Sonnet 5.5 by clicking the model name under the chat box. Sonnet uses less of your limit and still works well, but we recommend Opus 5.5 when you can. Please let us know it happened; it helps us a lot.

Something else went wrong

Take a screenshot of what you see and send it to the person who invited you. You can also just tell Claude what happened and ask it to help.

Who can see what I write?

Your entries are stored in your own Supabase account, not a shared database. The 167 Hours team can't see them. Your therapist sees only the summaries you choose to send.

167 Hours is not a crisis service. If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US), or call 911.
