# 167 Hours: Between Sessions

There are 167 hours between therapy sessions. This is for them.

Talk to Claude about your week, the way you already would. The important parts are saved quietly to your own private database and turned into a short summary for your therapist before each session.

**The one rule: you need a therapist.** This supports therapy and never replaces it.

**Not a crisis service.** If you're in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911.

## What's included

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| setup | Confirms you're 18+ and in therapy, builds your private database in your own Supabase account, and sets up your profile. Say "set up 167 Hours". |
| journal | Listens and saves what matters: thoughts, feelings, urges, wins, sleep, people, patterns, and what helped. Just talk. |
| session-notes | Right after therapy, captures what you covered and any homework. Say "debrief my session". |
| safety-plan | A short plan for really hard moments, with 988 always included. Say "show my safety plan". |
| weekly-report | Builds your therapist's summary. You review it first and send it yourself. Say "build my report". |

## Requirements

- A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).
- A free Supabase account, connected to Claude.

## Privacy

Your entries live in your own Supabase account, not a shared database. The 167 Hours team can't see them. Say "keep that private" about anything you don't want in your therapist's summary.
