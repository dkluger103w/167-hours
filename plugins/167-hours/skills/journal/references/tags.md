# Standard tags

Tag every entry with two to five of these, lowercase. Prefer these over new ones so the weekly report can group and count consistently. People go in the `people` table, not in tags. Patterns go in the `patterns` table, though a matching tag (like `spiral`) is fine too.

**Life areas:** work, money, house, family, relationship, dating, breakup, friendship, health, legal, school, parenting

**Feelings:** anger, anxiety, sadness, grief, shame, loneliness, fear, relief, hope, joy

**Inner patterns:** self-worth, self-talk, catastrophizing, spiral, rumination, rejection, boundaries, people-pleasing, isolation

**Recovery and urges:** urge, sobriety, relapse, craving

**Body:** sleep, exercise, eating, pain

**Care:** therapy, homework, coping, support, win
