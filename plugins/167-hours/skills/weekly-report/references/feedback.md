# Therapist feedback note

Add this at the very end of every report, after the footer, as a short separate paragraph:

> **For the therapist:** 167 Hours is in early testing, and we'd value your view. What was useful here, what was missing, and would you want this for other clients? No client details needed.
> Share feedback: FEEDBACK_LINK

If FEEDBACK_LINK above has not been replaced with a real web address, leave out the "Share feedback" line and instead end with: "Your client can pass your feedback along to the 167 Hours team."
